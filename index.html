Hushvault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version: Hushvault keeps your files on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud service of ours, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your files, your passwords or your browsing.

Who we are

Hushvault is published by Paul McElhinney, Australia. In this policy "we" means the publisher and "the app" means Hushvault for iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account sign-up, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send us any information about you or how you use it. We operate no server that receives your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Vault files. Photos, videos, scans and saved website links you put in the Vault are encrypted and stored only on your iPhone. They are excluded from iCloud backup.
  • Your Vault password. We never see it and cannot reset it or recover it for you.
  • Docs and receipts. Everyday documents, receipts and warranty details are stored on your iPhone, outside the Vault. If you use Apple's own iPhone backup, Apple may include app data in it under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.
  • Settings. Choices such as theme and search engine are stored on your iPhone.

Browsing

When you browse in the app, your iPhone connects directly to the websites you open and to the search engine you choose. Those sites receive what any browser sends them, such as your internet address, and their own privacy policies apply. We do not see or record your browsing.

Camera and Face ID

The camera is used only when you scan a document or receipt. Face ID, if you turn it on, is handled by your iPhone; the app only learns whether the check passed.

Reminders

Warranty reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server of ours.

Backups and exports

If you save a backup or export a file, the copy goes where you choose, such as a USB drive or the Files app. Backups are encrypted with the backup password you set. Exported files are ordinary copies and are no longer protected by the Vault. If you choose a location that syncs online, that provider's terms apply.

Purchases

Plans are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your card details. The app only learns from Apple whether a plan is active. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your iPhone, deleting files in the app, or deleting the app itself, removes them. We hold nothing to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
